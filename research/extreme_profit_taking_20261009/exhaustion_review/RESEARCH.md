# Exact-option premium exhaustion: sources and frozen research methods

Reviewed 9 October 2026, IST. Research only; no production, settings, position or ledger changes.

**Controlling evaluated rules:** [exhaustion_analysis/PLAN.md](../exhaustion_analysis/PLAN.md) and its [detector.py](../exhaustion_analysis/detector.py) define the actual frozen experiment. The candidate examples below are source synthesis, not its implementation specification. In particular, the evaluated divergence uses OHLC **highs**, not closing-price pivots; detector A requires a later band re-entry **and** a close below the preceding bar's low; arming has the declared ten-bar/session limits and refresh/supersession behavior. Use PLAN.md for every exact threshold, timing, cohort and accounting convention.

The objective is to detect an increased risk of giving back an observed option gain. A future maximum is knowable only in hindsight. RSI, MACD, bands and sentiment can supply hypotheses; none identifies the maximum with certainty. Applying equity-chart guidance to short-lived option premiums is an inference requiring exact-contract validation.

## Feature definitions and observation timing

- Use the exact held exchange/underlying/expiry/strike/CE-or-PE contract. For a long CE **and** a long PE, the favorable premium move is upward. Never splice ATM replacements or rolled contracts into the oscillator history. Use the strategy's existing bar interval and enough genuine pre-entry bars; do not fabricate or forward-fill missing bars. Separate signal-price quality from executable exit quality.
- Let `p_t` be the completed exact-option closing premium. Research based on trade OHLC must be labelled a trade-price diagnostic. A quote-based prospective version can use synchronized midpoint bars for indicators, but values liquidation at the available bid, including size, age, fees and slippage. Do not mix midpoint and last-trade history.
- `RSI14_t`: Wilder-smoothed gains and losses of `p_t`, with smoothing weight `1/14`. Keep the initialization and zero-loss convention fixed. `MACD_t = EMA12(p)_t - EMA26(p)_t`; `hist_t = MACD_t - EMA9(MACD)_t`. MACD is unbounded, so a raw MACD number is not a universal extreme across option premiums. RSI and MACD derive from the same prices and are not independent confirmations.
- For a transparent volatility feature, use `z_t = (p_t - mean(p[t-20:t])) / std_population(p[t-20:t])`, with the slice containing the **20 preceding** completed bars. The denominator must be positive. This deliberately uses lagged bands. `z >= 2` describes an extension relative to recent premiums; it is not a normal-distribution tail probability.
- A closing-price pivot high at bar `i` requires `p_i` strictly greater than `p_(i-2), p_(i-1), p_(i+1), p_(i+2)`. It is first known at the **close of i+2**, not at `i`. Store both `pivot_time` and `available_at`; plot actionable markers at `available_at`. Using strict comparisons resolves equal-high ties by detecting no pivot. Unfinished bars and unconfirmed higher-timeframe indicators are unavailable inputs.
- Running premium profit and running peak must use only observations already available at `t`. Final trade MFE, the session maximum, final daily volume/OI and subsequently revised data cannot enter a live trigger. A completed-bar trigger can execute only on the next eligible quote; neither the signal candle high nor a retrospective pivot price is an assured fill.

## Two candidates only; no parameter search

Keep entries and existing hard-stop, session and expiry exits fixed. Evaluate these as separate overlays against the unchanged baseline. Conventional indicator settings below are frozen research choices, not optimal or validated values.

1. **Premium excursion and re-entry.** After entry, arm on the first completed bar with `z_t >= 2`, `RSI14_t >= 70` and positive net liquidation P&L. Once armed, signal at the first completed bar with `z_t < 2`, `p_t < p_(t-1)` and positive net liquidation P&L. The falling premium condition avoids treating band widening alone as a reversal. Extreme strength alone never exits. The arm persists only for the current trade.
2. **Confirmed premium/RSI divergence and structure break.** For two adjacent confirmed closing-price highs `i < j` formed after entry, arm only when `p_j > p_i`, `RSI14_j < RSI14_i`, and `max(RSI14_i, RSI14_j) >= 70`. Save the intervening trough `min(p_(i+1), ..., p_(j-1))`. Signal on the first completed close at or after `j+2` below that trough while net liquidation P&L is positive. Log whether `hist_j < hist_i` and whether MACD crosses below its signal; do not add these as extra optimized gates. Replace the setup if a newer adjacent confirmed high pair forms, and clear it when the trade closes.

First compare full exits to isolate signal usefulness. A later whole-lot partial-exit experiment must account for realized cash and remaining quantity; a one-lot position cannot sell half a lot. No rule can guarantee retained profit through quote gaps or failed execution.

## Sentiment and Greeks: context until timestamped history exists

Define a fixed contract universe by underlying, expiry and moneyness before calculating sentiment. Never change included strikes because a later price move makes the chart look better.

- Window volume PCR: `sum(put volume increments) / sum(call volume increments)` for that fixed universe and a declared completed window. Handle cumulative-volume resets explicitly; a zero denominator is missing, not infinity. OI PCR uses `sum(put OI) / sum(call OI)` at the snapshot's actual availability time. Volume PCR and OI PCR are different features.
- OI change is participation, not the identity or intent of an aggressor: every contract has a buyer and seller. Do not label higher OI as fresh directional writing without opening/closing and trade-side evidence. Raw PCR mixes hedges, spreads and directional trades. Cboe documents US exercise-related nondirectional PCR spikes; that specific American-option mechanism must **not** be assumed for NSE European contracts.
- A skew feature can be `IV(25-delta put) - IV(25-delta call)` for the same expiry and synchronized surface, with interpolation and missing-leg handling fixed. This prices asymmetric risk and demand; it is not a timed forecast of an option-premium top. Never substitute Cboe's 30-day SPX SKEW for Nifty's intraday option-chain skew.
- Log exact-contract IV, delta, gamma, theta, expiry minutes and moneyness, plus their changes if reliable snapshots exist. Near-expiry ATM gamma can make premium responses highly nonlinear; accelerating theta and falling IV can reduce gains even while the underlying remains favorable. These are explanations and regime features, not sufficient standalone sell signals. IV inferred from the same premium is partly a transformation of that premium, not independent evidence. NSE's displayed IV is reference-only and periodically recomputed, so record its timestamp and vendor calculation method.
- If synchronized historic chains, quote ages, Greeks or sentiment are absent, mark them unavailable. Do not reconstruct intraday sentiment from end-of-day snapshots or impose a US OI publication schedule on Indian feeds. Use broker/exchange `available_at`, not merely the market timestamp. Add sentiment to prospective logging before testing it as a trigger.

## Validation boundaries

Report each candidate's triggered/missed trades, net incremental P&L, maximum drawdown, giveback, largest-winner contribution, extra costs, and behavior by CE/PE, expiry and volatility regime. Use identical starting cash/inventory and entries; distinguish isolated same-entry overlays from a full portfolio simulation where early exits affect later entries. Preserve adverse/no-trigger cases. Run chronological evaluation and then genuinely new paper observations, recording all rule trials. A better hindsight capture ratio or win rate is insufficient if total net returns or the strategy's large-winner tail deteriorates.

## Primary sources

1. [Fidelity RSI guide](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/RSI): RSI extremes can persist during strong trends; divergence and failure swings are possible reversal evidence, not certainty. Equity guidance does not establish an option-premium edge.
2. [Fidelity MACD guide](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/macd): conventional 12/26/9 construction; range-market whipsaws; divergence is stronger when accompanied by a crossover. MACD is not normally an overbought/oversold oscillator.
3. [Fidelity Bollinger Bands guide](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/bollinger-bands): bands adapt to dispersion; strong trends can hug/exceed them; prompt re-entry weakens breakout evidence. This supports testing re-entry, not selling merely because premium is high.
4. [TradingView repainting documentation](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/repainting/): realtime indicators change until bar close; backdated pivot plots can conceal the confirmation delay. [Pivot Extension Strategy](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000599888-pivot-extension-strategy/) explicitly states the right-bar detection lag.
5. [OIC gamma](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/gamma), [theta](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/theta), and [volatility/Greeks](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/volatility-the-greeks): gamma is greatest around ATM near expiry; time decay accelerates; vega links premium to IV. These are local model sensitivities, not exact future-price forecasts.
6. [OIC general information](https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/general-information): OI alone is neither bullish nor bearish, and OI/volume do not guarantee liquidity. The discussion of OCC end-of-day reporting describes US operations.
7. [Cboe: exercise flow and PCR](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/how-early-exercise-order-flow-impacts-equity-option-put-call-ratios/): demonstrates composition-driven nondirectional ratio extremes and the value of separating products/account types. US American-option example, not direct NSE evidence.
8. [Cboe SKEW white paper](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/indices/documents/SKEWwhitepaperjan2011.pdf), pp. 7–10: SKEW concerns risk-adjusted/risk-neutral 30-day SPX tail pricing. It is not an empirical intraday turning-point probability.
9. [NSE option-chain notes](https://www.nseindia.com/option-chain): displayed IV is for reference and computed periodically using inputs including LTP and underlying value. Contract quantities and snapshot timing matter.
10. [OIC bid/ask and execution](https://www.optionseducation.org/news/understanding-the-bid-and-ask-prices-for-options): spreads and order type affect slippage; limit orders may not fill. Historical last-trade extrema are not assured exit proceeds.
11. [Bailey et al., Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf): selecting configurations creates false-discovery risk; a holdout alone does not account for all attempted trials. Maintain a complete trial register and prospective validation.
