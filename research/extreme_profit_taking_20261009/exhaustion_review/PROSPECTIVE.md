# Independent conceptual assessment and prospective evidence

Reviewed the frozen [PLAN.md](../exhaustion_analysis/PLAN.md) and [detector.py](../exhaustion_analysis/detector.py), 9 October 2026. This note proposes instrumentation, not another exit policy or a threshold search. The controlling plan, rather than the illustrative candidates in RESEARCH.md, governs the evaluated results.

## Conceptual caveats of the two exact rules

**A — prior-20 premium extreme and subsequent failure.** Lagged reference bands, closed-bar RSI, a later band re-entry and a close through the preceding bar's low provide an explicitly available sequence. This is a plausible weakening pattern, not a known top. A small prior standard deviation can make an ordinary tick move look extreme. RSI and bands both summarize premium prices, so they are correlated evidence. The failure condition can arrive after material profit giveback, miss a gap reversal, or sell a temporary pause before continuation. Repeated extremes refresh the ten-bar clock: the setup can persist much longer than ten bars from its first arm, although it cannot cross a session boundary. Expiration of the arm can leave a later reversal unprotected by this overlay. These are expected tradeoffs to report, not reasons to alter thresholds after seeing outcomes. [Fidelity bands](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/bollinger-bands), [Fidelity RSI](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/RSI).

**B — higher OHLC high, weaker momentum, confirmed neckline failure.** Two right bars delay pivot availability; the neckline can already be broken when confirmation arrives, making confirmation the earliest decision. OHLC high is an intrabar trade extremum, whereas the attached RSI/MACD values use that bar's final close. This is a divergence between bar-level observations, not necessarily the momentum measured at the instant of the high. An isolated high print can create a pivot; positive total bar volume does not establish depth or liquidity at that high. The 0.2% difference has different tick significance at cheap and expensive premiums. The RSI-or-MACD branch is one prespecified composite rule, not two independent votes. The code requires the earlier MACD histogram to be positive, but permits the newer histogram to be zero or negative; describe this accurately rather than requiring both to be positive. A new consecutive pivot pair supersedes an older setup even if the new pair fails to qualify. Only the newer pivot must start after entry: the earlier reference may predate entry. Legitimate overnight gaps preserve indicator/pivot history; reference pairs may therefore span sessions, while armed decisions are confined to the confirmation session. Expiry/time-decay and overnight changes can affect that comparison. [TradingView pivot timing](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000599888-pivot-extension-strategy/), [Fidelity MACD](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/macd).

**Shared limits.** Both rules wait for weakness and can sacrifice the very large winners a trend strategy needs. Neither promises a fill or preserved gain. The residual-profit gate is estimated using trade close and declared costs; a later bid, inadequate depth or gap can erase the apparent net profit. Complete candles and positive volume cannot establish executable quotes. Indicators are deterministic transformations of the same exact-contract premium, whose path also reflects IV, gamma and theta. Three prior sessions and 100 complete bars improve context but do not establish stationarity or independent validation. Retrospective cohort selection, excluded open/addition lifecycles and absence of untouched holdout data limit conclusions. [OIC Greeks](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/volatility-the-greeks), [OIC execution](https://www.optionseducation.org/news/understanding-the-bid-and-ask-prices-for-options), [Bailey et al.](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf).

## Point-in-time prospective logging

Keep the two declared rules frozen in paper shadow observation. Preserve actual strategy decisions and record hypothetical detector decisions separately. Logging must not itself place orders or revise past observations. The following is a schema/design suggestion, not implemented production work.

| Record | Fields needed to interpret the observation later |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and provenance | Exact exchange, instrument token, underlying, expiry, strike, option type, lot size and tick size; lifecycle ID; source/feed; schema and detector versions; snapshot/revision IDs. |
| Time and validity | Exchange event time, local receipt time, first `available_at`, aggregation bar start/end, source age and clock status. Keep revisions as later records. Preserve missing/stale/invalid reasons rather than substituting zero or neutral. |
| Executable contract market | Bid/ask prices and available quantities, depth if supplied, last-trade price/time/quantity, cumulative volume and reset markers, OI value and its reported/received times. Sample the existing feed and preserve a market snapshot at each detector decision; do not assume a candle close was a tradable quote. |
| Pricing state | Synchronized spot/futures quote, exact-contract IV and delta/gamma/theta/vega if supplied, expiry minutes and moneyness. Distinguish exchange/broker/model-derived fields. For modeled values, save model/version and input quote, underlying, interest/dividend and day-count conventions. |
| PCR/OI/skew context | Underlying and expiry scope; explicit strike/moneyness membership; call and put numerator/denominator components; volume-window boundaries or OI snapshot times; OI-change reference; missing contracts. Save same-expiry 25-delta IV legs/interpolation inputs if skew is available. Keep the vendor's whale/composite score and component/version separately. |
| Trade-flow context | Source trade IDs/times/quantities and synchronized bid/ask if supplied; signing method/version and ambiguous/unclassified volume. Buying at ask is a trade-side heuristic, not proof of opening intent, institutional identity or a new bullish bet. Do not infer signed flow from OHLCV. |
| Other sentiment | Source identity, coverage, publication time, first-observed time and processing/model version for news, breadth or derived sentiment. Distinguish an unavailable feature from an observed neutral score. |
| Detector audit | Entry and remaining whole lots; prior actual sale IDs; completed feature values; arm/refresh/expiry/supersession times; pivot time **and** confirmation availability; both divergence components; neckline and exact trigger/no-trigger reason; estimated costs/profit and contemporaneous bid/depth. |

For optional five-minute context joins, choose the newest snapshot whose **availability** is no later than the decision and whose age satisfies the declared limit. Never select a later snapshot merely because its nominal window timestamp precedes the decision. Preserve source scope: validated NIFTY/BANKNIFTY context does not supply stock-option sentiment. Reconcile component timestamps within a chain snapshot; a recently stored window can still contain old quotes or OI.

Do not turn OI increases into directional writing or use volume PCR interchangeably with OI PCR. Cboe's nondirectional exercise-flow example concerns American US options; it illustrates composition risk, not a claimed NSE mechanism. NSE labels its displayed IV as reference data calculated periodically, so freshness and methodology must remain visible. IV computed from the same premium is not independent corroboration. [OIC OI guidance](https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/general-information), [Cboe PCR example](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/how-early-exercise-order-flow-impacts-equity-option-put-call-ratios/), [NSE chain notes](https://www.nseindia.com/option-chain).

Keep a complete trial register and the original detector version. Append future outcome labels only after their observation horizon has elapsed, retaining both actionable decision time and hindsight peak time. Assess executable incremental net P&L, missed continuation and giveback on new observations; do not choose a sentiment gate or report a maximum-move probability from the already inspected outcomes.
